AppliQ Privacy Policy

When you use AppliQ, you trust us with your job application history and career journey. We are committed to maintaining that trust with transparent privacy practices and giving you complete ownership over your data.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

AppliQ is built on the principle of Zero-Surveillance Job Tracking. Unlike traditional recruiting aggregators that monetize candidate databases, AppliQ never sells, rents, brokers, or analyzes your personal application data for commercial advertising.

Your job applications, salary expectations, interview notes, and resume attachments are strictly scoped to your authenticated account and are never shared without your permission.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to provide you with job application tracking, calendar reminders, and cloud synchronization:

Google Account Identity

Your full name, email address, and profile photo synchronized securely via official Google OAuth 2.0.

Job Application Records

Company names, job roles, salary ranges, locations, pipeline stages (Applied, Interview, Offering), and your notes.

Resume & Cover Letter Data

Education, employment history, certifications, hard/soft skills, contact links, and formal cover letter preferences used solely to compile your PDF documents.

Digital Signatures & Documents

Optional digital signature images and CV attachments stored in your account storage or designated Google Drive folder for document rendering.

3. Why AppliQ Processes Your Data

  • Provide Application Tracking & Kanban Pipelines: To display your pipeline timeline, calculate progress statistics, and organize interview stages.
  • Generate ATS Resumes & Formal Cover Letters: To dynamically format, preview, and render downloadable career documents directly on your device.
  • Schedule Local & Calendar Reminders: To sync interview appointments with Google Calendar and trigger notifications prior to scheduled sessions.
  • Export Custom Reports: To generate PDF and CSV export files on-demand for your personal offline records.

4. Keeping Your Data Secure

We employ standard industry security controls to safeguard your information:

Row-Level Security (RLS)

Database queries are isolated at the database engine level so only your authenticated Google UID can read or modify records.

Encryption in Transit

All communication between the mobile app, Google Drive API, and Supabase servers is encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.

5. Exporting & Deleting Your Information

You maintain total authority over your personal information at all times.

Permanent Account Deletion

You can delete your account permanently inside the app via Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. When confirmed, all your application logs, stage history, and user profile are immediately wiped from the cloud database.
