Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At AppliQ, your job search privacy is our highest priority. Your application history, salary expectations, interview notes, and CV attachments are strictly confidential and never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Profile Data: Display name, email address, and profile photo synchronized during sign-in.
  • Job Application Details: Job title, company name, salary range, progress status (Applied, Interview, Offering, etc.), and personal notes.
  • Interview Schedules: Date, time, video call links, and interviewer notes for local notification alarms.
  • Resume & Portfolio Files: Document links stored securely in your own Google Drive storage.

3. Security & Data Protection

All data transmitted between the client application and server uses industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption. Our cloud database enforces strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring only the authenticated account owner can read or write their records.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to export or permanently delete your account and all related data at any time directly within the app (go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account). Upon confirmation, your data is erased immediately and irreversibly.

5. Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at support.appliq@gmail.com.
